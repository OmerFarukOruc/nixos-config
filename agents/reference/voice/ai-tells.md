# AI tells: phrases and patterns to remove

Curated from Josh's AI-writing-tells deny-list, the stop-slop skill, and observed Claude-family habits. Phrases
can be matched by grep; patterns need judgment.

## Phrases (grep-able)

Classic vocabulary: delve, tapestry, testament to, meticulous, seamless,
cutting-edge, boasts, realm, landscape (figurative), journey (figurative),
game-changer, unlock (figurative).

Significance inflation: plays a pivotal role, plays a key role, marks a
pivotal moment, a significant milestone, crucial first step, vital
component.

Hedging stacks: could potentially, it could be argued, it is worth noting,
it is important to note, arguably.

Filler and throat-clearing: here's the thing, let's dive in, here's the
kicker, at the end of the day, the truth is, it turns out, in today's world,
in the world of.

Chatbot artifacts: I hope this helps, certainly!, great question, as an AI,
I appreciate your patience, absolutely! (as an opener).

Future optimism: the future looks bright, watch this space, exciting times
ahead, endless possibilities.

Emphasis crutches: full stop., let that sink in, plot twist:, spoiler:,
make no mistake.

## Patterns (judgment)

- Negative parallelism, the number one tell: "It's not X; it's Y", "The
  answer isn't X. It's Y." State what it is directly.
- Tricolon habit: three parallel items in every list and sentence. Two is
  usually enough; four is fine when true.
- Staccato fragment stacking sold as punchy: "That's it. That's the
  system." Write the sentence.
- Sandwich conclusion: restating the intro as the outro. End when the
  content ends.
- Rhetorical question then answer: "So what does this mean? It means..."
  Delete the question, keep the answer.
- Metronomic rhythm: every sentence 15 to 25 words, every paragraph the
  same shape. Vary or it reads generated.
- Perfectly parallel bold-led bullets in every list. Use bullets for real
  lists only.
- Anthropomorphic punchline: "the archive already knew". Systems do not
  know things.
- Mannered prose: see voice.md, Plain sentences.
- Claude habits to watch in ourselves: hedging by default, "I appreciate"
  openers, any em dash, over-neat internal coherence.
